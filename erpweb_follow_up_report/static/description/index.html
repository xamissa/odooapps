Aging Bucket Follow up Report

Features:

The Aging Bucket Follow up Report module extends Odoo's native accounting follow-up capabilities by providing advanced aging bucket configuration and customizable partner balance reporting. This powerful tool enables businesses to better manage their accounts receivable by organizing outstanding invoices into configurable aging periods and generating detailed follow-up reports.

Configurable Aging Buckets

Define custom aging periods (0-30 days, 31-60 days, etc.) to match your business requirements and credit policies.




Aged Receivable: After configuring company-specific settings, you can view the standard Odoo built-in reports according to each company’s configuration.




Aged Payable: After configuring company-specific settings, you can view the standard Odoo built-in reports according to each company’s configuration.
